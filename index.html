About our school

Al Noor Public School was founded in 1998 with one simple idea: children learn best when they feel safe, known and curious. Today more than 1,500 students learn with us, guided by a team of caring teachers who treat every classroom as a community.

We balance strong academics with arts, sports and life skills, so students leave school ready for college, careers and everyday life.

Admissions open for 2027–28

Applying takes four simple steps. Our admission team is happy to guide you at every one of them.

  1. Fill the enquiry form. Tell us your child's name and the class you are applying for.
  2. Visit the school. Take a campus tour and meet our principal and teachers.
  3. Interaction round. A friendly chat with your child. Classes 6 and above also take a short written assessment.
  4. Confirm the seat. Submit documents and pay the admission fee to secure your child's place.

Before you apply

  • Nursery: age 3+ years as on 31 March 2027
  • Birth certificate and Aadhaar copy
  • Previous report card and transfer certificate (Class 1 and above)
  • 4 passport-size photographs of the child

Applications close on 31 January 2027. Seats fill quickly, so please apply early.
